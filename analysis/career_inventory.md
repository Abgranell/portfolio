## 4. Scientific Publications

### 4.1 PRISM: Personalized Refinement of Imitation Skills for Manipulation via Human Instructions
- **Authors:** **Arnau Boix-Granell** (First Author), Alberto San-Miguel-Tello, Magí Dalmau-Moreno, Néstor García.
- **Venue:** European Robotics Forum (ERF 2026).
- **Preprint / Identifier:** arXiv:2603.05574 (`https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05574`).
- **Media Demonstration:** Video available at `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yUUkD1LsCWY`.
- **Abstract & Significance:** Proposes PRISM, a prompt-conditioned imitation policy refinement method bridging Imitation Learning (IL) and Reinforcement Learning (RL) into a unified pipeline. Starting from user-guided demonstrations, the generic policy is refined through RL using an extension of the Eureka framework (LLM-driven iterative reward generation from natural language). By incorporating real-time human feedback during intermediate rollouts, PRISM adapts to unseen goal configurations and constraints while dramatically reducing sample complexity and computational burden.
- **Primary Contribution:** Conceptual design, IL/RL pipeline formulation, simulation environment in Isaac Lab, LLM prompt engineering, and empirical benchmarking.

### 4.2 A Virtual Reality Framework for Fast Dataset Creation Applied to Cloth Manipulation with Automatic Semantic Labelling
- **Authors:** Júlia Borràs, **Arnau Boix-Granell**, Sergi Foix, Carme Torras.
- **Venue:** 2023 IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA 2023), London, UK.
- **DOI:** `10.1109/ICRA48891.2023.10161122`.
- **Repository Links:** IEEE Xplore (`https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10161122`) | DIGITAL.CSIC (`http://hdl.handle.net/10261/354962`).
- **Abstract & Significance:** Addresses the fundamental challenge of teaching dual-arm robots to fold garments by providing an immersive Unity-based VR framework for human demonstration. Captures human visual feedback loops while bypassing physical cloth state tracking limitations. Introduces a novel low-dimensional semantic labeling representation to distinguish cloth topological configurations automatically.
- **Primary Contribution:** Framework development, VR interactive environment, sensor integration (HTC Vive + Leap Motion), data acquisition, and semantic labeling implementation.

### 4.3 Garment Manipulation Dataset for Robot Learning by Demonstration Through a Virtual Reality Framework
- **Authors:** **Arnau Boix-Granell** (First Author), Sergi Foix, Carme Torras.
- **Venue:** 24th International Conference of the Catalan Association for Artificial Intelligence (CCIA 2022).
- **Publication:** *Artificial Intelligence Research and Development*, Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence and Applications, Vol. 356, IOS Press, pp. 199–208, 2022.
