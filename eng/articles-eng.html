PRISM: Personalized Refinement of Imitation Skills for Manipulation via Human Instructions

Conference Paper

Authors: Arnau Boix-Granell, Alberto San-Miguel-Tello, Magí Dalmau-Moreno, and Néstor García

Imitation Learning Reinforcement Learning LLM Prompting NVIDIA Isaac Sim Isaac Lab Eureka Paradigm Human-in-the-Loop Python

Abstract: This paper presents PRISM: a prompt-conditioned imitation policy refinement method for robotic manipulation. This approach integrates imitation learning (IL) and reinforcement learning (RL) into a unified workflow, allowing a generic imitation policy generated from user-guided demonstrations to be refined through reinforcement to generate new, previously unobserved detailed behaviors. The refinement process builds upon the Eureka paradigm, where reward functions for RL are generated iteratively from natural language task specifications. The proposed method extends this mechanism to adapt a refined imitation policy to new target configurations and incorporate constraints via human feedback corrections during intermediate executions. Results on a dual-phase pick-and-place task in simulated physics demonstrate that PRISM dramatically improves success rates and computational sample efficiency over open-loop and unassisted baselines.

A Virtual Reality Framework for Rapid Dataset Generation Applied to Garment Manipulation with Automatic Semantic Labeling

Flagship Conference

Authors: Júlia Borràs, Arnau Boix-Granell, Sergi Foix, and Carme Torras

Virtual Reality (VR) Deformable Object Manipulation HTC Vive Pro Unity 3D Automatic Semantic Labeling Learning from Demonstration (LfD) C++ MATLAB

Abstract: Teaching complex manipulation skills, such as folding garments, to a dual-arm robot is a challenging problem often addressed through learning from demonstration (LfD). Available garment-folding datasets have traditionally been restricted by either the perceptual difficulty of capturing deformable object states from human demonstrations, or the kinematic unreality of open-loop simulation lacking human visual feedback. In this paper, we present an accurate dataset of human garment-folding demonstrations collected through our novel virtual reality framework based on Unity 3D and the HTC Vive Pro system. The immersive platform allows realistic physics simulation of textiles and real-time controller interaction, closing the human-in-the-loop perceptual feedback loop while capturing ground-truth mesh states. The resulting garment manipulation sequences are automatically and semantically labeled with a compact representation that classifies transitions between key folding topological configurations.

CCIA 2022 · IOS Press FAIA · 1st Author

Garment Manipulation Dataset for Robot Learning from Demonstration Using a Virtual Reality Framework

Conference Paper

Authors: Arnau Boix-Granell, Sergi Foix, and Carme Torras

Robot Learning from Demonstration Cloth Manipulation Virtual Reality (VR) Unity 3D Engine HTC Vive Pro C++

Abstract: Teaching complex bimanual manipulation skills to robots requires rich, multi-modal demonstration datasets. In this paper, we present a compact yet high-fidelity dataset of human garment-folding trajectories collected using an immersive virtual reality platform. Implemented in Unity 3D with the HTC Vive Pro tracking system, the environment reproduces realistic textile mechanics and enables natural handheld interaction. By bridging the gap between human perception and robotic action spaces, the framework eliminates occlusion artifacts typical of optical motion capture while ensuring physically feasible grasping trajectories for dual-arm robotic systems. Published in Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence and Applications (IOS Press, Vol. 355).
