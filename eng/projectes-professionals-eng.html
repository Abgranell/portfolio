GRAIL: General Robot Architecture for Intelligent Learning

Eurecat [2026 - Present]
Development of learning-based robotic manipulation systems
Topics: VLM VLA Agentic AI

Contributions:
• Integration of reasoning models based on Vision-Language Models (VLM) to interpret and coordinate natural language instructions.
• Integration of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models into physical robotic platforms for instruction-based autonomous control.
• Implementation of teleoperation systems for robotic demonstrations to train AI models.

Summary: GRAIL is an initiative focused on advancing architectures for robotic intelligence, combining state-of-the-art learning methods to enable robots to generalize complex manipulation tasks autonomously and collaboratively in real environments.

INTELLIMAN: AI-Powered Manipulation System for Advanced Robotic Service, Manufacturing and Prosthetics

Eurecat - [2022-2026]
Development of intelligent manipulation for industrial and domestic processes
Topics: RL IL Sim2Real IsaacLab IsaacSim ROS2

Contributions:
• Training reinforcement learning (RL) models to perform complex manipulation tasks related to the domestic domain.
• Study of Sim2Real techniques to successfully transfer simulation-trained models to real robotic platforms.
• Use of imitation learning models to pre-train reinforcement learning models and accelerate convergence.
• Adaptation of 3D object tracking models and their integration to work alongside RL models.

Summary: INTELLIMAN is a European research project focused on equipping robots with intelligent, agile, and adaptive manipulation capabilities based on artificial intelligence, aiming to solve complex manipulation tasks both in circular economy-oriented industrial environments and domestic scenarios.
