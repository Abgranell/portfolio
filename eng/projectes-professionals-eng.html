COGNITIUM: Autonomous & Integrated Cognition in Multimodal Environments

Eurecat · MWC 2026 [2025 – 2026]
Robotics Researcher · Physical Embodiment on PAL Robotics TIAGo PRO
PAL Robotics TIAGo PRO Hugging Face LeRobot VLA Fine-Tuning Agentic VLM Reasoning ROS 2 Live MWC 2026 Demo

The Challenge: Deploying autonomous mobile manipulators in unconstrained, crowded human environments requires overcoming perceptual errors, visual occlusions, and unpredictable physical disturbances. Traditional cascading architectures (decoupled SLAM, task planning, and motion planning) are notoriously brittle. COGNITIUM implemented an integrated cognitive architecture combining Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models with an agentic Vision-Language Model (VLM) supervisor to achieve robust, real-time closed-loop manipulation on a full-scale mobile robot.

Arnau's Direct Contributions:
• VLA Benchmarking & Selection: Conducted a rigorous comparative benchmark of open-source and proprietary VLA architectures, evaluating inference frequency, spatial generalization, and sample efficiency.
• LeRobot Pipeline Integration: Adapted the Hugging Face LeRobot framework to interface directly with the low-level motor controllers of the physical PAL Robotics TIAGo PRO mobile manipulator.
• Physical Policy Deployment: Fine-tuned sensorimotor policies on demonstration datasets and achieved autonomous grasp and placement of varied target objects directly from onboard RGB-D camera feeds.
• Agentic VLM Supervision: Built a high-level VLM supervisory agent acting as an executive reasoner to evaluate execution outcomes, detect grasping slips, and trigger autonomous recovery actions.
• Live MWC 2026 Showcase: Programmed, calibrated, and conducted live physical manipulation demonstrations at Mobile World Congress Barcelona 2026 under real-world auditorium interference and dynamic lighting.

Results & Impact: Validated real-time closed-loop VLA manipulation running natively on the physical TIAGo mobile manipulator with automated visual error recovery, presenting Eurecat's cutting-edge cognitive robotics capabilities to thousands of international industry leaders.

PRISM: Personalized Refinement of Imitation Skills for Manipulation via Human Instructions

Lead Author · ERF 2026 [arXiv:2603.05574]
Arnau Boix-Granell, Alberto San-Miguel-Tello, Magí Dalmau-Moreno, Néstor García
Imitation Learning (IL) Reinforcement Learning (RL) Eureka LLM Reward Synthesis NVIDIA Isaac Lab Human-in-the-Loop 96.8% Success Rate

The Challenge: Imitation Learning (IL) policies trained from human demonstrations suffer from poor out-of-distribution generalization: when goal poses, obstacles, or user preferences shift, the policy fails. Conversely, retraining Reinforcement Learning (RL) agents from scratch requires manual reward engineering and immense compute. PRISM creates a unified pipeline where a pre-trained imitation policy is rapidly personalized and refined through natural language task prompts and human feedback rollouts without manual reward design.

Arnau's Direct Contributions:
• Conception & Architecture: Formulated PRISM as lead investigator and first author, bridging Imitation Learning priors with prompt-conditioned RL policy refinement.
• Eureka Reward Synthesis Extension: Extended the state-of-the-art Eureka paradigm using GPT-5 to iteratively synthesize, evaluate, and optimize code-level Python reward functions directly from natural-language task instructions.
• Human-in-the-Loop Feedback: Implemented real-time corrective interventions allowing human supervisors to guide intermediate rollouts and enforce physical constraints.
• Isaac Lab Simulation Benchmarks: Programmed GPU-accelerated parallel simulation environments in NVIDIA Isaac Lab, conducting extensive empirical evaluations under severe distribution shifts.

Empirical Benchmarks & Metrics:
• Zero-Shot Baseline: Generic unrefined imitation policies achieved only 21.2% success when deployed on out-of-distribution goal configurations.
• Autonomous Isaac Lab RL: Training in Isaac Lab elevated task convergence to 98.0%.
• PRISM Framework: Combining iterative GPT-5 reward generation with interactive human feedback achieved 96.8% empirical success while dramatically lowering sample complexity and rollout requirements.

GRAIL: Generative Robotics & AI for EU Industrial Leadership

EU Horizon Europe Flagship [2026 – Present]
Robotics Researcher · Eurecat (Consortium Coordinator with Siemens, ETH Zurich, DLR, Imperial)
Horizon Europe €40M Flagship VLA Foundation Models Agentic VLM Reasoning Industrial Manipulators Teleoperation Pipelines

The Challenge: European industrial automation requires high-mix, low-volume flexibility that rigid, pre-programmed robotic lines cannot support. Achieving industrial sovereignty requires foundation AI models capable of decomposing complex natural language production commands, reasoning over sensor streams, and executing robust continuous manipulation without manual trajectory engineering.

Arnau's Direct Contributions:
• Agentic VLM Reasoning Modules: Designed reasoning pipelines utilizing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to interpret high-level natural language instructions and break them down into structured symbolic sub-goals and spatial manipulation primitives.
• VLA Physical Embodiment: Integrated Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation neural models onto physical robotic manipulators, mapping RGB-D camera feeds directly to continuous joint commands.
• Teleoperation Data Pipelines: Developed low-latency teleoperation setups to record high-fidelity human demonstration rollouts (proprioception, visual tokens, contact states) required for generative policy pre-training.
• Safety Verification: Implemented real-time boundary-checking mechanisms to enforce strict collision-avoidance and physical workspace limits during generative model inference.

Results & Impact: Delivering sovereign embodied AI architectures for the €40M Horizon Europe GRAIL initiative, enabling autonomous execution of multi-step industrial assembly instructions without hand-crafted robot code.

INTELLIMAN: AI-Powered Manipulation for Service, Manufacturing and Prosthetics

EU Horizon Europe Consortium [2022 – 2026]
Robotics Researcher · Eurecat (Consortium led by Univ. of Bologna, partnering with DLR, IIT)
Franka Emika Panda NVIDIA Isaac Lab NVIDIA Isaac Sim Sim2Real Domain Randomization Deep RL (PPO) Imitation Pre-Training ROS 2

The Challenge: Enabling robotic arms to interact dynamically with complex articulated household appliances (dishwashers, cabinets) and fragile industrial goods requires adapting to continuous friction variations, mass distributions, and non-rigid mechanical latches. Direct physical reinforcement learning is dangerously slow and damages hardware, while policies trained purely in simulation typically fail in the real world due to the Sim2Real dynamic gap.

Arnau's Direct Contributions:
• GPU Parallel Simulation: Developed high-fidelity dynamic manipulation environments in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab simulating a 7-DOF Franka Emika Panda arm interacting with articulated appliances.
• Imitation-Accelerated RL: Overcame exploration bottlenecks in contact-rich latch manipulation (opening dishwasher doors) by pre-training RL policies with Imitation Learning trajectories, dramatically accelerating convergence.
• Domain Randomization: Engineered extensive randomization of physical parameters (friction coefficients, link inertias, joint damping, spring latch thresholds) to achieve robust Sim2Real policy transfer.
• Real-Time 3D Perception: Coupled 6D object pose tracking with ROS 2 sensor pipelines, delivering continuous spatial states to the policy under real-world visual occlusions.

Results & Impact: Demonstrated zero-shot transfer of learned manipulation policies from Isaac Lab onto physical Franka Panda manipulators, delivering core milestones on circular economy and domestic manipulation for the EU Horizon Europe INTELLIMAN project.
