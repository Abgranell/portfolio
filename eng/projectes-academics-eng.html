September 2023 – January 2025 · Defended January 31, 2025

Reinforcement Learning for Complex and Dynamic Manipulation Tasks

Grade: 10/10 with Honors (Matrícula d'Honor)

Master's Thesis: Double Master's Degree in Automatic Control & Robotics Engineering and Industrial Engineering (UPC ETSEIB & Eurecat) · Advisors: Dr. Néstor García Hidalgo & Prof. Jan Rosell Gratacòs

Reinforcement Learning (RL) NVIDIA Isaac Sim Isaac Lab ROS 2 Franka Emika Panda Sim2Real Transfer Domain Randomization PyTorch

The Engineering Challenge: Teaching non-prehensile dynamic manipulation skills—such as unlatching and pulling open a sprung dishwasher door—is notoriously challenging. Closed kinematic chains, friction transitions, and latch release dynamics cause standard position controllers to jam or fail. While Reinforcement Learning (RL) in simulation provides an adaptive path forward, overcoming the simulation-to-reality (Sim2Real) gap remains the central bottleneck for physical hardware execution.

Arnau's Direct Contributions:

  • Engineered a high-performance simulation environment in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab, leveraging GPU tensor parallelization to train thousands of RL agents concurrently.
  • Accurately modeled joint kinematics, spring resistance, and contact discontinuities of a commercial dishwasher mechanism.
  • Formulated curriculum reward functions enabling a 7-DOF Franka Emika Panda robot to coordinate end-effector compliance and pulling forces without losing grasp.
  • Applied domain randomization (mass, friction, actuator delays) to harden learned policies against unmodeled real-world dynamics.
  • Established an asynchronous ROS 2 bridge connecting simulation-trained policies to physical robotic hardware for real-world validation.

October 2024 · Col·legi d'Enginyers Industrials de Catalunya · Team InduBotics

Engihack 2024: EcoShower

1st Prize Winner (€3,000 Grand Prize)

Engineering Hackathon: 12-hour intensive innovation competition · Broadcast live on Catalan national television network (**TV3**, *"Tot es mou"*)

Mechatronic Design Automated Bypass Valving Thermostatic Control Techno-Economic Modeling Rapid Prototyping Technical Pitch Defense

The Sustainability Challenge: Millions of liters of domestic potable water are wasted daily while waiting for residential showers to reach target temperature. Within an intense 12-hour engineering hackathon organized by the Association of Industrial Engineers of Catalonia, our team engineered EcoShower: an automated closed-circuit fluid interception mechanism.

Arnau's Contributions: Designed the hydraulic bypass circuit with motorized solenoid valves and thermostatic sensing logic to divert sub-target temperature water into domestic utility reservoirs before releasing heated water to the showerhead. Modeled water and energy savings across standard multi-unit residential buildings, and co-delivered the executive technical defense. Awarded 1st Prize (€3,000) among all competing teams and interviewed on TV3.

October 2021 – July 2022 · Defended July 2022 · IRI CSIC-UPC & UPC ETSEIB

Development of a Learning from Demonstration Environment Using ZED 2i and HTC Vive Pro

Grade: 10/10 with Honors (Matrícula d'Honor)

Bachelor's Thesis: Industrial Technologies Engineering (UPC ETSEIB & Institut de Robòtica i Informàtica Industrial, CSIC-UPC) · Advisors: Dr. Sergi Foix, Dr. Júlia Borràs & Prof. Carme Torras

Virtual Reality (VR) Learning from Demonstration (LfD) Cloth Manipulation HTC Vive Pro Stereolabs ZED 2i Leap Motion Unity 3D C++

The Core Innovation: Designed and implemented an immersive Virtual Reality platform in Unity 3D integrating the HTC Vive Pro, Stereolabs ZED 2i / ZED Mini stereo depth cameras, and Leap Motion hand tracking. The platform realistically simulates deformable textile dynamics in real time, allowing human operators to fold and interact with garments using natural hand-eye feedback while automatically capturing ground-truth 3D mesh states without physical camera occlusions.

Academic & Scientific Impact: Awarded a perfect 10/10 with Honors (*Matrícula d'Honor*), this thesis directly led to first-author and co-author publications at IEEE ICRA 2023 (London) and CCIA 2022, establishing a 19-sequence open-access garment manipulation dataset.
