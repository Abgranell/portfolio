Reinforcement Learning for Complex and Dynamic Manipulation Tasks

January 2025
Master's Thesis of the Double Master's Degree in Industrial Technologies and Automatic Control and Robotics Engineering
Topics: RL IsaacSim IsaacLab ROS2

Summary: Teaching complex skills, such as opening a dishwasher, to robotic manipulators is a very difficult task often approached through direct programming. This technique is suitable for controlled environments where variables such as relative positions or physical properties are known and constant, but its performance decreases in less controlled environments, reducing repeatability and success rate. Furthermore, in some cases, direct programming is not viable. In this thesis, advanced simulation tools NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab are used to develop a new environment that allows training reinforcement learning (RL) agents to carry out complex dynamic manipulation tasks. Initially, basic tasks are explored to become familiar with the tools and progressively adjust simulation parameters, laying the foundations of reinforcement learning to develop more advanced environments. Techniques to reduce the simulation-to-real gap (Sim2Real), a key obstacle to deploying simulation-trained models in real-world environments, are also studied. Subsequently, these techniques are incorporated into the developed system and a Franka Emika Panda robotic manipulator is trained to open a dishwasher door in a simulated environment. Finally, a connection between simulation and the real world is established using ROS 2, allowing the reproduction of one of the simpler tasks to be tested on a real robot.

Development of a Learning from Demonstration Environment Using a ZED 2i and an HTC Vive Pro

July 2022
Bachelor's Thesis of the Bachelor's Degree in Industrial Technologies Engineering
Topics: IL Unity ROS2 VR C++

Summary: Development of a virtual reality environment based on Unity, HTC Vive Pro, ZED Mini and ZED 2i cameras, and the Leap Motion hand-tracking system to facilitate robotic learning from demonstration. The environment allows detecting and tracking objects, animals, and human bodies in 3D, as well as simulating realistic garments and interacting with them in real time using controllers or user hands. The system simplifies data collection and yields more realistic demonstrations for robotic manipulation task learning. Finally, it is used to create a new garment manipulation dataset featuring samples and videos of 19 different types of manipulation.
