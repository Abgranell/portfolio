Reinforcement Learning for Complex and Dynamic Manipulation Tasks

Master's Thesis · Grade: 10/10 with Honors
Double Master's Degree in Automatic Control & Robotics and Industrial Engineering (UPC ETSEIB & Eurecat) · Advisors: Dr. Néstor García Hidalgo & Prof. Jan Rosell Gratacòs
Reinforcement Learning (RL) NVIDIA Isaac Lab NVIDIA Isaac Sim Franka Emika Panda Sim2Real Transfer ROS 2 10/10 Honors

The Challenge: Teaching non-prehensile dynamic manipulation skills, such as unlatching and pulling open a sprung dishwasher door, is notoriously difficult. Closed kinematic chains, friction transitions, and latch release dynamics cause standard position controllers to jam or fail. While Reinforcement Learning (RL) in simulation provides an adaptive path forward, overcoming the simulation-to-reality (Sim2Real) gap remains the central bottleneck for physical hardware execution.

Contributions:
• GPU Simulation Framework: Built a high-performance simulation environment in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab, leveraging GPU tensor parallelization to train thousands of RL agents concurrently.
• Articulated Mechanism Modeling: Accurately modeled joint kinematics, spring resistance, and contact discontinuities of a commercial dishwasher mechanism.
• Curriculum Reward Formulation: Formulated curriculum reward functions enabling a 7-DOF Franka Emika Panda robot to coordinate end-effector compliance and pulling forces without losing grasp.
• Sim2Real Domain Randomization: Applied domain randomization (mass, friction, actuator delays) to harden learned policies against unmodeled real-world dynamics.
• ROS 2 Hardware Bridge: Established an asynchronous ROS 2 bridge connecting simulation-trained policies to physical robotic hardware for real-world validation.

Results & Impact: Successfully trained a robust RL policy capable of dynamically approaching, latching, and opening a simulated dishwasher door across diverse initial conditions, validating zero-shot transfer of simpler policies on a physical Franka Panda robot arm. Defended January 31, 2025 at UPC ETSEIB with a grade of 10/10 with Honors (Matrícula d'Honor).

AIxHRI Summer School: Artificial Intelligence for Human-Robot Interaction

Doctoral School · Inria / Loria [Nancy, France]
Advanced doctoral training in Foundation Models, LLMs/VLMs, Imitation Learning, RLHF, and VLA physical deployment
Foundation Models Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Imitation Learning RLHF Physical Robot Deployment PyTorch ROS 2

Curriculum & Research Focus: Advanced doctoral school bringing together European PhD students and leading researchers in artificial intelligence and robotics. The curriculum combined theoretical and hands-on workshops in foundation models (LLMs, VLMs), interactive policy refinement via human evaluative feedback (RLHF), and the physical deployment of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models onto actual physical robotic manipulators and mobile bases.

Contributions:
• Hands-on VLA Implementation: Fine-tuned and deployed vision-language-action policies directly on physical collaborative robot arms.
• Human-in-the-Loop Alignment: Programmed interactive RLHF policy correction routines adapting trajectories from human guidance.
• Semantic Grounding: Investigated architectures mapping linguistic instructions to low-level continuous action manifolds.

Engihack 2024: EcoShower

1st Prize Winner (3,000 EUR Award) · Col·legi d'Enginyers Industrials
12-hour engineering hackathon · Team InduBotics · Featured on Catalan national television (TV3, "Tot es mou")
Mechatronic Design Automated Bypass Valving Thermostatic Control 1st Prize (3,000 EUR) TV3 News Broadcast

The Challenge: Millions of liters of domestic potable water are wasted daily while waiting for residential showers to reach target temperature. In an intense 12-hour engineering competition organized by the Association of Industrial Engineers of Catalonia, our team engineered EcoShower: an automated closed-circuit fluid interception mechanism.

Contributions:
• Hydraulic Circuit Architecture: Conceptualized the closed-circuit interception bypass with motorized solenoid valves to redirect cold water to auxiliary utility reservoirs.
• Thermostatic Sensing Logic: Specified sensor selection and electronic logic enabling showerhead release only once target temperature is attained.
• Techno-Economic Modeling: Quantified water and energy savings across urban multi-unit residential buildings, calculating payback periods.
• Executive Pitch Defense: Co-delivered the technical pitch and defense before the industrial jury.

Results & Impact: Awarded 1st Prize (3,000 EUR grand prize) among all competing teams and interviewed on TV3 ("Tot es mou").

Development of a Learning from Demonstration Environment Using ZED 2i and HTC Vive Pro

Bachelor's Thesis · Grade: 10/10 with Honors
Degree in Industrial Technologies Engineering (UPC ETSEIB & IRI CSIC-UPC) · Advisors: Dr. Sergi Foix, Dr. Júlia Borràs & Prof. Carme Torras
HTC Vive Pro VR Learning from Demonstration (LfD) Cloth Manipulation Stereolabs ZED 2i Leap Motion Unity 3D Engine C++ Led to ICRA 2023

The Challenge: Capturing high-quality demonstration datasets for deformable object manipulation is obstructed by camera occlusions in physical setups and artificial kinematics in open-loop simulations lacking human visual feedback.

Contributions:
• VR Platform Architecture: Engineered an interactive Virtual Reality environment in Unity 3D integrating the HTC Vive Pro, Stereolabs ZED 2i / ZED Mini stereo depth cameras, and Leap Motion hand tracking.
• Deformable Physics Modeling: Implemented real-time textile mechanics enabling natural grasping and folding with handheld controllers.
• Ground-Truth Recording: Captured complete 3D mesh states without physical occlusion while preserving natural human hand-eye visual feedback.
• Automated Semantic Labeling: Developed algorithms classifying folding topological states across 19 manipulation sequences.

Results & Impact: Awarded a perfect 10/10 with Honors (Matrícula d'Honor) at UPC ETSEIB. Formed the foundation for publications presented at IEEE ICRA 2023 (London) and CCIA 2022.
