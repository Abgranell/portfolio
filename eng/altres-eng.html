euRobotics Diversity and Inclusion Initiative (DII)

European Working Group

Organization: euRobotics AISBL (The European Association for AI, Data and Robotics) · European Robotics Forum (ERF) Working Group

Community Leadership European Robotics Forum (ERF) Ethics in AI & Robotics Diversity & Inclusion Workplace Accessibility STEM Outreach

Context & Core Mission: The euRobotics Diversity and Inclusion Initiative (DII) promotes an equitable, open, and representative ecosystem across European robotics research and industry. As autonomous systems enter healthcare, manufacturing, and domestic human-shared environments, ensuring that the engineers designing these technologies reflect the full diversity of society is essential to mitigating algorithmic bias, guaranteeing physical safety for all demographics, and accelerating public trust.

Arnau's Direct Contributions:

  • European Robotics Forum (ERF) Engagement: Actively participates in organizing and coordinating workshops, panels, and community discussions at the annual ERF to address systemic barriers to entry, retention, and career progression in robotics engineering.
  • Ethical Frameworks: Collaborates with European research and industry peers to formulate actionable guidelines ensuring robotics systems are developed ethically, safely, and equitably for diverse user populations.
  • Inclusion Advocacy: Champions initiatives bridging academia, tech centers, and industry to cultivate pathways for underrepresented groups in robotics and STEM.
